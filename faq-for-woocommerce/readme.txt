== Description ==

**Happy FAQs** is the **all-in-one FAQ plugin** for WordPress and WooCommerce: **Site FAQs** for any page and **Product FAQs** for your store, in one plugin. Create unlimited FAQs in one library and show them as a beautiful accordion wherever your visitors and customers have questions.

**Site FAQs**: create general FAQs once and display them anywhere: a FAQ page, a one-click Help Center, posts, Gutenberg, Elementor, Divi, or a shortcode. **Product FAQs**: add FAQs to products, product categories, and tags, let customers ask and answer each other with Product Q&A, and show FAQs on shop, cart, and checkout pages (PRO). WooCommerce is optional: start with site FAQs, then add product FAQs when you open a store.

= How it works =

Create a FAQ once. On that FAQ screen, the **Happy FAQs Panel** metabox is where you choose where it appears.

1. Go to **Happy FAQs → Add New**. The title is the question. The editor is the answer (HTML, images, and video are allowed).
2. Optionally assign a **FAQ category** (for example Shipping or Returns). You can later show that whole category on a help page.
3. Open the **Happy FAQs Panel** metabox and assign:
   * **Products** — search and attach specific WooCommerce products.
   * **Product categories** — every product in those categories inherits this FAQ.
   * **Product tags** — every product with those tags inherits this FAQ.
   * **Display in pages** — product pages (free). Shop, cart, and checkout are (PRO).
   * **Global FAQs (PRO)** — show this FAQ on every product.
   * **Product attributes (PRO)** — insert live values such as `{product_price}` in the answer.
   * **Nested FAQs (PRO)** — show other FAQs inside this answer as a smaller accordion.
4. To show FAQs on a normal WordPress page, use a shortcode, **Happy Prebuilt FAQs** or **Happy Custom FAQs** (Gutenberg block, Elementor widget, or Divi 4/5 module), or **Settings → FAQ page**.

You can also attach FAQs from the product edit screen. One FAQ can appear in more than one place.

= Shortcodes =

[Shortcode docs](https://happydevs.net/docs/faq-for-woocommerce/shortcodes/)

Show **all FAQs**:

`[ffw_template]`

Show a **FAQ category**. `faq_cat` is the FAQ category ID (Happy FAQs → Categories):

`[ffw_template faq_cat="32"]`

Show **selected FAQs**. `faq_ids` are FAQ post IDs (Happy FAQs → All FAQs):

`[ffw_template faq_ids="10,11,12"]`

Show FAQs for a **WooCommerce product**:

`[ffw_template id="20"]`

`id` is the WooCommerce **product ID**, not the FAQ ID. Find it under **Products → All Products**: hover the product, or open it and read `post=20` in the browser URL. Replace `20` with your product’s ID.

Show FAQs for the **current product** (use this on a single product page or product template):

`[ffw_template dynamic_post="true"]`

Pick an accordion layout with `template`:

* 1 Classic, 2 Whitish, 3 Trip, 4 Pop, 5 Basic
* Pro layouts (PRO): 6 Glow, 7 Smart, 8 Wow, 9 Zoom

Example: `[ffw_template template="1" id="20"]`

Sort with `order` (`ASC` or `DESC`) and `order_by` (`id`, `date`, `title`, `name`, `modified`, `comment_count`, `author`).

Add a **category filter** above the FAQs:

`[ffw_template filter="true"]`

Show **5 FAQs, then a "Show more" button**:

`[ffw_template per_page="5"]`

Show **only 5 FAQs** in total:

`[ffw_template limit="5"]`

Choose a **toggle icon**, its position, color, and size:

`[ffw_template icon="plus-minus-circle" icon_position="left" icon_color="#4f46e5" icon_size="20"]`

Icon names: `chevron`, `chevron-bold`, `chevron-circle`, `chevron-solid`, `chevron-square`, `chevron-right`, `double-chevron`, `arrow`, `arrow-circle`, `arrow-right`, `caret`, `caret-right`, `caret-circle`, `plus-minus`, `plus-minus-light`, `plus-minus-circle`, `plus-minus-solid`, `plus-minus-square`, `plus-close`, `plus-close-circle`, `folder`, `switch`, plus `default` (the layout's own icon) and `none`.

Attributes can be combined, for example `[ffw_template faq_cat="32" filter="true" per_page="5"]`.

Pro adds `group_id` for FAQ Groups (PRO), and `[ffw_search]` for AJAX library search (PRO).

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is this a WooCommerce FAQ plugin or a WordPress FAQ plugin? =

Both. Happy WooCommerce FAQs is a WordPress FAQ accordion plugin. Product FAQs work when WooCommerce is active. You can use it without WooCommerce for site FAQs.

= Do I need WooCommerce? =

No. Use it as a site FAQ plugin. WooCommerce is only needed for product FAQs and store extras.

= Does it work with any theme? =

Yes. It works with standard WordPress themes.

= How do I add a product FAQ? =

Go to **Happy FAQs → Add New**, write the question and answer, then in the **Happy FAQs Panel** metabox search and select products. You can also assign product categories or tags so many products inherit the same FAQ. Or open a product and attach FAQs there.

= How do I show FAQs on a normal page, not a product? =

Paste `[ffw_template]` in the page, insert **Happy Prebuilt FAQs** (library) or **Happy Custom FAQs** (write Q&A on the page) as a Gutenberg block, Elementor widget, or Divi 4/5 module, or pick a page in **Happy FAQs → Settings → FAQ page**.

= How do I find the product ID for the shortcode? =

Go to **Products → All Products**. Hover the product, or edit it and look at the URL: `post=20` means the product ID is `20`. Use `[ffw_template id="20"]`.

= Can I assign product categories and tags to FAQs? =

Yes, in the free version. Every product in that category or tag can inherit those FAQs.

= Can I show FAQs on shop, cart, checkout, or archive pages? =

Product pages are included in free. Shop, cart, checkout, and product archive locations are Pro.

= Does it output Google FAQ schema? =

Yes. FAQPage schema is included for help pages and product pages, with duplicate questions removed. It helps search engines and AI answer engines understand your questions and answers.

= Can I make a help center page? =

Yes. Go to **Happy FAQs → Help Center Page**. Choose filter tabs or a category sidebar, add a most asked questions section and a contact button, then create a draft or publish. Happy FAQs Pro adds a search across every FAQ and an "Ask a question" form.

= Are there ready-made FAQ designs? =

Yes. Happy FAQs includes **21 ready-made FAQ patterns**. Open **Happy FAQs → Patterns** to see live previews, then click **Create page**, or insert them in the block editor from **Patterns → Happy FAQs**. Each pattern has real starter questions you can edit. Pick a look in the block's **Styles** panel: Cards, Minimal, or Soft.

= Can I use Gutenberg, Elementor, or Divi? =

Yes. Free includes **Happy Prebuilt FAQs** and **Happy Custom FAQs** as a Gutenberg block, Elementor widget, and Divi 4/5 module. Prebuilt shows FAQs from your library. Custom lets you write questions and answers on the page.

= Can I import FAQs from a spreadsheet? =

Yes, with Happy FAQs Pro. Go to **Happy FAQs → Import / Export**, add a CSV or Excel file (or paste cells from Google Sheets), check the matched columns, review the changes, and import. You can undo an import afterwards. **Download CSV** exports your FAQs to edit in a spreadsheet and import again.

= Can I put FAQs inside another FAQ's answer? =

Yes, with Happy FAQs Pro. Open a FAQ and pick other FAQs in the **Nested FAQs** row of the Happy FAQs Panel. They show inside its answer as a smaller accordion, in the order you drag them to, up to three levels deep. Good for long help topics with follow-up questions.

= Can FAQs answer people before they contact us? =

Yes, with Happy FAQs Pro. Turn on **Contact Form Suggestions** in Settings. While visitors type in a WPForms, Contact Form 7, or Fluent Forms contact form, matching FAQs appear under the message, and they can read the answer without leaving the form. **Analytics** shows how many tickets were avoided.

= Can I write HTML or add media in answers? =

Yes. Answers use the WordPress editor, so you can add HTML, images, and video.

= Can I sort FAQs? =

Yes. Drag FAQs on a product, or use `order` and `order_by` on the shortcode.

= Can I change the open and close icon? =

Yes. Choose from **22 toggle icon sets** in **Settings → Style**, with a preview of the closed and open state. Set the icon position, color, and size there too. Each block, Elementor widget, or shortcode can pick its own icon, for example `[ffw_template icon="plus-minus"]`. Icons work with every layout.

= Do I need an OpenAI key for AI FAQs? =

No, not on WordPress 7.0 or later. Connect an AI provider once in **Settings → Connectors** and Happy FAQs uses it. On older WordPress, or if you prefer, add your own OpenAI key in **Happy FAQs → Settings → AI FAQs**. Generated FAQs are always shown for review before they are saved.

= How many accordion layouts are there? =

Five in free (Classic, Whitish, Trip, Pop, Basic). Four more in Pro (Glow, Smart, Wow, Zoom).

= What is the difference between inline search and AJAX library search? =

Inline search (free) filters the FAQs already on the page. AJAX library search (Pro) searches all published FAQs and can be placed anywhere with `[ffw_search]`.

= Can visitors filter FAQs by category? =

Yes. Add `filter="true"` to the shortcode, or turn on **Show category filter** in the Happy Prebuilt FAQs block or Elementor widget. Visitors see one button per FAQ category.

= Can I show a few FAQs and load the rest on click? =

Yes. `[ffw_template per_page="5"]` shows five FAQs and a **Show more FAQs** button. The block and Elementor widget have the same option. Hidden FAQs still appear in search, the category filter, and FAQ schema.

= Can I see which FAQs are helpful or most viewed? =

Yes. Turn on **Helpful Votes** in Settings to show "Was this helpful? Yes / No" under each answer. **All FAQs** shows sortable Views and Helpful columns. IP addresses are not stored; repeat votes are blocked with a short-lived anonymous hash. Happy FAQs Pro adds an **Analytics** dashboard with trends, top searches, and searches that found no answer.

= Does it work with LMS plugins? =

Yes, with Happy FAQs Pro. Tutor LMS, LearnDash, and LearnPress courses and lessons get their own FAQs, assigned the same way as product FAQs. They show after the course or lesson content, or where you place `[ffw_lms_faqs]`.

= Are the FAQ accordions accessible? =

Yes. Every layout works with the keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space), shows a visible focus outline, and tells screen readers whether an answer is open or closed.

= Does it support right-to-left languages? =

Yes, in the free version. **Settings → Right to Left (RTL)** follows your site language automatically, or you can set it to always or never.

== Changelog ==

= 3.0.0 =
* Added: A new admin. Every Happy FAQs screen is rebuilt with a slim header, and its actions work without page reloads: Dashboard with a setup checklist and live counts, Settings with search and a live style preview, a shortcode builder with a live preview, Templates with live previews of your FAQs, AI FAQs, Analytics, Help Center page builder, Patterns, Customer Questions and the setup wizard. Saved settings and data are unchanged, and each previous screen is still there (add `&ffw_classic=1`).
* Added: One FAQs screen with tabs for All FAQs, Add New, and Categories, with no page reloads. Quick add, quick edit, bulk actions, duplicate, where each FAQ is shown, and the editor in place. The old screens are still there (add `&ffw_classic=1`).
* Added: FAQ pattern library: 21 ready-made FAQ sections with live previews, a Patterns gallery, one-click pages, and 3 block styles (Cards, Minimal, Soft).
* Added: One-click Help Center page builder with filter tabs or a category sidebar, a most asked questions section, and a contact button.
* Added: 22 toggle icon sets with position, color, and size controls, site-wide or per block, widget, and shortcode.
* Added: AI FAQs use the AI provider connected in WordPress (Settings → Connectors), with no extra API key. Write FAQs for products, pages, or topics, and Improve with AI in the FAQ editor.
* Added: Most viewed and newest FAQ lists.
* Added: Dashboard Free vs Pro comparison and supported page builders.
* Improved: Divi 5 module ships in the production build.
* Fixed: Plugin Check errors.
* Fixed: "Before the FAQs" and "After the FAQs" content showed its HTML as text on the site.
* Fixed: The two shortcode switches in Settings now say what they do: they hide [ffw_template] lists on the site.
* Fixed: Clicking formatted text inside a Pop layout answer closed the answer.
* Fixed: A FAQ whose answer contains the `[ffw_template]` shortcode made the page run out of memory. A FAQ list inside an answer now leaves that FAQ out and shows its own answers only.
* Fixed: Opening a Classic or Pop FAQ that contains another FAQ list opened every answer in that list.

= 2.1.0 =
* Added: FAQ category filter. Use `filter="true"` or the new block and Elementor option.
* Added: "Show more" button for long lists (`per_page`), and `limit` on the shortcode.
* Added: "Was this helpful?" votes and answer view counts, with sortable Views and Helpful columns on All FAQs.
* Added: Right-to-left (RTL) support in the free version, automatic for RTL site languages.
* Improved: Accessible accordions in every layout: keyboard support, aria-expanded state, and a visible focus outline.
* Improved: CSS and JS now load only on pages that show FAQs, for the layouts in use.
* Improved: Inline search now works with every layout.
* Fixed: Shortcodes using a layout other than the default (for example Pop or Basic) did not open.
* Fixed: A Pop layout answer could not be closed once opened.
